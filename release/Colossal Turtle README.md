# Colossal Turtle 0.1.0

A mod for Minecraft Java 1.21.1 with Fabric. An old turtle about 6,000 blocks long (nearly 10,000 with his head and
tail) walks through every world. From far off he looks like a mountain range. He's made of real blocks, and you can
climb on and live on his back.

## Installing

You need Fabric for 1.21.1 and Fabric API. Put `colossal-turtle-0.1.0.jar` in `.minecraft\mods`, and in the
server's mods folder too if you play on one. Anyone joining needs it as well.

`install.ps1` in the repo folder puts the newest jar in your mods folder and takes the old one out.

## Finding him

He starts about 4,500 blocks from spawn and just keeps walking. `/turtle` tells you where he is, which way he's
going and how far he is from you. He never goes more than about 20,000 blocks from spawn before turning back.

## Getting on

Walk into one of his feet, fly onto his shell or land on him with an elytra. Up there you're on his back for real:
build, mine, sleep in a bed. The world goes by underneath as he walks. Walk or fall off the edge and you're back in
the world where you dropped off.

## On his back

- **Shell Highlands**: big stone plates up top, snowy in the middle, cold and windy, with old ruins.
- **Moss Valleys**: wet green grooves between the plates.
- **Rim Forest**: old spruce trees round the edge of his shell, with roots and glow berry vines hanging off it.
- **Underhang**: the dark under his shell. Glowing lichen, dripstone and drips.
- **Leg Cliffs**: his legs, scaly stone with ledges, vines and turtle egg nests. Don't hang on one when it moves.
- **Neck Bridge** and **Head Lands**: out along his neck to his head, with two eye lakes and his nostrils, which
  breathe you in and out.
- **Tail Marsh**: mud and puddles behind.
- **Inside**: tunnels through his body, and his stomach, with the ships and ruins he swallowed.

## Moods

- **Calm**: walks about 25 blocks a second. Every step shakes the ground and leaves a footprint lake and flattened
  trees.
- **Scared**: during thunderstorms he walks faster and the quakes are bigger.
- **Tired**: after a few days he lies down at night and sleeps for one to three days.
- **Hiding**: blow things up on his back and he pulls his head, legs and tail into his shell for a few minutes.

## Commands for ops

- `/turtle ride` puts you on his back.
- `/turtle bring` puts him 6,000 blocks in front of you.
- `/turtle mood calm|scared|tired|hiding`
